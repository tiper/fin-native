# v0.2.0 — PlxNative 0.10.0: automatic intro skipping, yellow subtitles, Previous episodes, a player HUD you can replace, and ASS we can

This release moves FinNative onto PlxNative 0.10.0. It lets you choose, install and replace the player's on-screen controls, skips intros and credits automatically, adds a Yellow subtitle colour, draws styled ASS subtitles with their own fonts, and redesigns Settings, Home's hero and Continue Watching. Previous now plays the episode before, a card shows its video quality and HDR, a favourite changed on another device reaches Home at once, and a long list of seeks that hung now finish. It is the first release since 0.1.0, which was cut on 2026-11-03.

It is built on PlxNative 0.10.0 ([0158681](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/commit/015868116287136757e456797754fd9a846d42b4)), released on 12 November 2026. Settings > About names that base on every build.

## What's new

- **A player HUD you can choose and replace.** Settings → Video & playback → **Player HUD** lists the built-in HUD and any installed HUD packages, per profile. **Browse HUDs** opens a gallery of published HUDs with a preview and separate Install and Use buttons; a package can also be installed from the HUD folder on the television or from an address. The built-in HUD is now itself a package drawn by the same renderer, so Chapters and Info are sections of it, a **More** fold holds the rest, and menus open next to the control that opened them. A package names the PlxNative versions it works with, and FinNative judges that against its PlxNative base. How packages work is in PlxNative's [HUD plugins](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/blob/main/docs/hud-plugins.md) guide.
- **Skip intros and credits automatically.** Settings → Video & playback has per-profile **Skip intros** and **Skip credits** choices of Manual, Disabled or Automatic, for every Jellyfin viewer: the intro and credits markers are the ones your server stores for the item (its media segments). Automatic skipping waits five seconds of actual playback. On the timed prompt the filled button is the action the countdown performs (Skip Intro, or Next Episode after credits), pressing it does that now, and a separate Watch Intro or Watch Credits button beside it cancels the countdown.
- **Previous plays the episode before.** An episode's queue now carries the episode before it, so Previous plays it, wherever the episode was started from. **From beginning** on the player's Info tab starts the episode over.
- **When it will finish.** The player's HUD shows the local time the film or episode ends at; the **Estimated end time** switch on the Player HUD settings page is offered only while the HUD in use has room for it. Thanks to [seergi893](https://github.com/seergi893).
- **A Yellow subtitle color.** Settings → Audio & subtitles → Look has a **Subtitle color** row beside Size and Position, and the same choice is under Color in the player's Subtitles menu: White (as before), Yellow, or one of five grays that only dim. Yellow colors text subtitles (SubRip, WebVTT and the other text formats) and the white text of styled ASS and SSA subtitles; a cue's own other colors are kept. Picture subtitles (PGS, VobSub) never take it, and where the subtitle on screen cannot take a color the Yellow choice is dimmed with the reason. The cost: signs and other on-screen text a styled subtitle draws in white also turn yellow.
- **Settings, redesigned.** The root is four groups (Libraries, Playback, System, Support) with a sentence about the focused row, and Diagnostics is one row that opens its own page. **Under posters** now lets you tick up to four details per kind (movies: year, length, genre, video quality, age rating, score; shows: year, seasons, genre, age rating, score) with a two-poster preview.
- **Video quality and HDR on cards.** The Home hero and the details under posters now show a title's resolution (4K, 1080) and whether it is HDR or Dolby Vision, read from what your Jellyfin server stores for it.
- **A Home hero that tells you more, and Continue Watching as stills.** The hero shows playback progress in its Resume pill and, for every movie, show and episode, a line with the type, year, length, genres and age rating. Each Continue Watching tile is a wide 16:9 picture with the title's backdrop and logo, a one-line footer with an episode label and time left, and a play glyph on focus.
- **Library, reorganised.** The library list and **Favorite libraries** live in the Library options menu, Sort & Filter holds only sorting and filtering, and a Library remembers the page you left it on, per profile.
- **Changes from another device reach Home at once.** A favourite added or removed, or a Home setting changed, in another Jellyfin client refreshes FinNative's Home straight away, over the live connection FinNative already keeps to your server.
- **Catalan.** The interface is now also in Catalan ([seergi893](https://github.com/seergi893)), alongside the eleven of 0.1.0.

## Fixed

- **ASS we can.** Styled ASS and SSA subtitles with embedded fonts and heavy karaoke, such as anime openings, now use the fonts attached to the file, animate on every video frame instead of a few times a second, and a translucent sign over the picture is no longer drawn dark ([PlxNative issue #1328](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/1328)). Thanks to [josmellop](https://github.com/josmellop).
- **A Dolby Vision film that hung on Buffering after a resume or seek** now plays the HDR10 base layer when the resume point lands on a CRA picture ([PlxNative issue #1166](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/1166)).
- **A seek that reloads, or that never reached the video, left the picture on the old position.** Seeks on a stream the server converts and on an HTTP/2 stream are recovered, and Back saves the position you asked for.
- **Sound on a converted remux could disappear for long stretches** when the app threw away audio it should have played; it is now played.
- **Magic Remote Back and pointer recovery** after the player's controls closed: Back hides the pointer, and a second Back leaves playback ([PlxNative issue #811](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/issues/811)).
- **The Library grid did not refresh after a server change,** and opening the Library straight onto a saved page now puts focus on the first tile instead of the top bar.
- **Messages said "your Plex server".** The Dolby Vision setting, the player's conversion notes and the sign-in connection messages now name your Jellyfin server, in every language.

## Changed

- **FinNative asks your server for less.** Poster rows, seasons, collections, a show's page and its extras ask only for what they draw, and leaving Continue Watching takes fewer requests. The live connection to your server now runs on PlxNative's shared WebSocket client.
- **The Player HUD gallery contacts huds.plxnative.com,** a host PlxNative's project runs, to read its signed list of HUDs, and fetches previews without credentials; the list refreshes at most daily. Nothing is sent to it about you or your server.
- **Installed HUD packages are stored outside the app's own folder,** in `/media/developer/io.github.tiper.finnative-huds` on a Developer Mode television or the first of a few other locations the app can write to, and a package copied into that folder is offered for install. A package is a declarative description with SVG masks, not code, and the app checks it before drawing it.
- **A Playback engine setting.** Settings → Video & playback → **Playback engine** has Standard (the default, exactly as before) and Experimental. On webOS 5 and newer the list of sets Experimental may drive ships empty in this release, so it does not yet change how a video plays there.
- **Seeking shows the loading sign until the seek lands,** and the transport keeps its rewind and fast-forward arrows while you scrub.

## Compatibility

- **Plays video, verified by watching it — 2021 LG sets.** One television: an LG C1, which reports platform release 6.5.3. FinNative is developed on it; the audit says what was run on it.
- **Starts, and nothing further is known — every other firmware from webOS 4.0 up is expected to start.** This binary's libraries and symbols are checked against the firmware images webosbrew has; the result for this build is in the [technical audit](https://github.com/tiper/fin-native/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.2.0.md). That grades startup and says nothing about whether video plays.
- **Does not start — webOS 3.9.2 and older.** Symbols the app needs are missing there, so the process is killed before anything appears.

If your set is in the middle group, [tell us what happened](https://github.com/tiper/fin-native/issues). It working is as useful a report as it failing.

## Installing

Download **io.github.tiper.finnative_0.2.0_arm.ipk** and install it with [dev-manager-desktop](https://github.com/webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop). FinNative is not in the Homebrew Channel yet. You do **not** need a rooted TV. FinNative installs beside PlxNative; it is not affiliated with Jellyfin or PlxNative.

Nothing in this distribution chain is signed, so this sha256 is what tells you the file you have is the file published here:

```
__IPK_SHA256__  io.github.tiper.finnative_0.2.0_arm.ipk
```

[Installing and checking a download](https://github.com/tiper/fin-native/blob/main/docs/install-finnative.md) covers the other assets, how to check the hash on each platform, and the Developer Mode expiry that uninstalls your apps. This package bundles FFmpeg under LGPL-2.1-or-later and its complete corresponding source is attached to this release. Exactly what was built, verified and shipped is in the [technical audit for v0.2.0](https://github.com/tiper/fin-native/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.2.0.md).
